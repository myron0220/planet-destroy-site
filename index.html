Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Planet Destroy does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • The game has no account and no sign-in of its own.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, a copy of it is kept in your own iCloud account so it can follow you to your other devices. We cannot see it.
  • If you use Game Center, your scores and achievements are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We receive nothing from it.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • The game shows no ads, uses no analytics and contains no trackers.

You can delete your progress at any time under Settings → Delete save, which also removes the copy in iCloud.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 12 日

凿击星球不收集、不存储、不分享任何个人信息。

  • 游戏没有账号，也不需要登录。
  • 游戏进度保存在你的设备上。如果你登录了 iCloud，进度会在你自己的 iCloud 账户里另存一份，方便换设备时接着玩，我们看不到这些数据。
  • 如果你使用 Game Center，分数和成就由苹果按照苹果的隐私政策处理，我们不会收到任何信息。
  • 购买由苹果处理，我们看不到你的付款信息。
  • 游戏不显示广告，不做数据统计，不包含任何追踪。

你可以随时在「设置 → 删除存档」里删除进度，iCloud 里的那份也会一起删除。
